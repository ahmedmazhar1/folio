GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Can I order just a few pieces?

Minimum quantities vary by product and are shown in each product’s options. The listed minimum is a guide; availability and final production quantities need confirmation.

Is the price on the card my final price?

Prices come from EcoPak Online’s public product listings and match the selected option. They can change. Confirm current stock, taxes, shipping and the final total on the official EcoPak store. Custom printing is quoted separately.

Can I design every product in 3D?

Every catalogue product has a 3D preview. Published dimensions are used where available; missing measurements are clearly identified as proportional estimates. A production dieline and physical sample need approval before printing.

What happens when I finish my quote list?

Submit the enquiry form with your chosen products and requirements. You’ll receive an enquiry reference; the team will confirm availability, pricing and delivery before an order is agreed.

How should I prepare my artwork?

Keep an editable original and high-resolution artwork. Mockup PNGs are for presentation. For printing, confirm bleed, safe margins, colour profile and the production dieline with your printer before approval.
