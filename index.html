GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Can I order just a few pieces?

Minimum quantities vary by product and are shown in each product’s options. The listed minimum is a guide; availability and final production quantities need confirmation.

Is the price on the card my final price?

It’s a market guide for a specific plain or standard configuration, researched from Pakistani suppliers. Your final price depends on size, stock, quantity, printing and finishing. Tax and delivery are not included unless confirmed in your quote.

Can I design every product in 3D?

The live studios currently support selected box structures, a paper bag, hoodies and half- and full-sleeved shirts. Other products use a specification brief. Product illustrations show the idea, rather than a proof of the finished item.

What happens when I finish my quote list?

You can save and download a brief with your chosen products and requirements. This version does not take payment or place orders. Your brief stays in this browser until you choose to download or share it.

How should I prepare my artwork?

Keep an editable original and high-resolution artwork. Mockup PNGs are for presentation. For printing, confirm bleed, safe margins, colour profile and the production dieline with your printer before approval.
